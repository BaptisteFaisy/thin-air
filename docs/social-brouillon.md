## Le post (texte à copier)

#KiroweenHackathon26 Thin Air — play music with your hands in mid-air. A
webcam, a browser, six synthesized instruments, and a latency counter that
tells the truth: every part of the delay, labelled measured or estimated.
Notes snap to a scale, so a wrong note takes effort — unless you want the
ghost glide. Built by an agent that was never allowed to hear it: every
musical behaviour is a property test on recorded hand movements, rendered to
a file and measured. Nothing leaves your browser.

Site: https://tradoooo.github.io/thin-air/
